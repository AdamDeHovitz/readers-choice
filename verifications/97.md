# Verification: feat: join policies and requests to join

## Change

Admins choose who can join a club: invite only (unlisted, the default), approval required, or open. Non-members can ask to join (with a note) or join open clubs from the club page. Admins approve or decline requests on the Members tab, which shows a count. Declines are quiet, with a 30-day cooldown. Opening a club admits everyone waiting. An invite link still admits anyone. The club and invite actions are now operations.

## Not verified

- Joining with an invite link while a request is pending (it should clear the request): covered by `server/operations/invites.test.ts` only. The seed has an invite link only for Seed Club, and every non-member I used was already through it by then.
- The operations over `/api/v1` (mobile transport). The mobile app has no join screens yet, and I only drove the web transport.
- The `join_request_cooldown` refusal through the UI: the panel hides the form during the cooldown, so I checked the database function directly.
- The rate limit (20 join attempts an hour) against the real `consume_rate_limit`: covered only by a unit test with a stubbed result.
- Deployed code (main) against the new schema: left to CI's Migration compat job. The change is additive: a defaulted column, a new table and new functions.
